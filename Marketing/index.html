Where we stand, honestly

We're not here to claim we're winning a race. Here's exactly where Momento sits in the ambient-display landscape right now.

The direct threat is Gallery Mode: it already has personal media, collages, and a large install base. Momento's only hope is to be visually superior — a design engine, not a media player. That's the bet. It's also the point.

Non-negotiable

Your memories belong to you

  • Export and deletion controls are visible and simple.
  • Backup is encouraged, never hidden.
  • Account recovery always exists.
  • Guest uploads require host approval.
  • Unapproved uploads expire automatically.
  • File limits and rate limiting are enforced.
  • Explicit consent for all shared content.

Momento is never "the only copy" of your memories. It's a window into them.
